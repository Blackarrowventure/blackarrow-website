Radiation Safety & Shielding

Lead Sheets for Hospitals

We supply and install lead sheet radiation shielding for diagnostic and treatment rooms where X-ray, CT, or gamma radiation is in use. Shielding thickness is calculated per room from workload, occupancy, and distance factors, then fabricated and installed into walls, doors, and viewing windows — with post-installation survey testing to confirm the finished room meets exposure limits.

  • Radiation Shielding Design — lead-equivalent thickness calculated per room from workload, use, and occupancy factors
  • Lead Sheet Supply & Fabrication — custom-sized sheet lead for walls, doors, and lead-glass viewing windows
  • Post-Installation Survey Testing — leakage verification confirming the finished room meets exposure limits

Critical Care Power

Isolated Power Panels

Hospital-grade isolated power systems for operating rooms and critical care areas, keeping equipment energized through a single ground fault while the fault is detected and alarmed — engineered to meet NFPA 99 requirements for wet procedure locations.

  • Isolated Power Systems — supply and integration of hospital-grade isolated power panels for operating rooms and wet procedure locations
  • Line Isolation Monitoring — continuous ground-fault detection (LIM) that alarms on a single fault without interrupting power to life-critical equipment
  • Compliance-Driven Design — engineered to NFPA 99 and NFPA 70 requirements for healthcare wet procedure locations
