What Is a UPS System?

An Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) sits between the utility grid and your critical equipment, instantly switching to battery power the moment mains power fails or fluctuates outside safe limits. For mission-critical systems — servers, medical equipment, production lines, control rooms — even a fraction-of-a-second gap in power can mean lost data, halted processes, or equipment damage.

UPS systems range from small standby units protecting a single server rack to large online double-conversion systems supporting an entire data center or manufacturing floor. The right topology and capacity depend on your load profile, required runtime, and how much power quality protection your equipment needs beyond simple outage backup.
