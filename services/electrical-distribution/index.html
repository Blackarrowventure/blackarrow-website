Power Infrastructure

What Electrical & Power Distribution Covers

Power distribution is the layer between the utility connection and every load in a building — main distribution boards (MDB), sub-distribution boards (SMDB), final distribution boards (DB), switchgear, and the control panels (MCC, PLC, SCADA) that automate and protect the system. Getting this layer right determines whether a facility runs reliably or suffers nuisance trips and downtime.

We design distribution systems around your actual load schedule and future expansion plans, not just present-day demand, and size protection devices, cabling, and containment (trays, conduits, busbars) to match — so the system has headroom rather than running at its limit from day one.
