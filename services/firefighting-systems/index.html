What Our Firefighting Services Cover

A complete fire protection system combines detection (smoke, heat, and gas sensors tied to an addressable alarm panel), suppression (sprinklers, clean-agent systems, or water mist depending on the risk), and manual intervention equipment (hydrants, hose reels, extinguishers) — all designed to work together during an actual event, not just pass an inspection.

The right combination depends on occupancy type and fire risk: a server room needs clean-agent suppression that won't damage equipment, a warehouse needs sprinkler density matched to stored commodity class, and a high-rise needs standpipe and hose reel systems integrated with evacuation planning. We design to the actual risk, not a generic checklist.
