Consultancy for Airport Facilities

Plan upgrades that keep operations running, with the cost laid out.

Consultancy

Airports

Airport facilities cannot simply be switched off for a project. The consultancy looks at the systems that keep a terminal or airfield running and plans upgrades in stages that respect live operations.

What It Covers

What the consultancy covers

Resilient power

UPS, distribution and backup arrangements for critical airport loads.

Aviation and facility lighting

Obstruction, helipad and general lighting reviewed for performance and upkeep.

Fire protection

Detection and suppression suited to terminals, plant rooms and support buildings.

HVAC for large spaces

Cooling strategy for terminals and public areas, with running cost in view.

Phased delivery under live operations

A staged plan that limits disruption and shows the cost of each phase.

The exact scope depends on your site and is agreed in writing before work starts.

What You Receive

A plan you can act on

01

Site and requirements review

We review your site conditions, drawings and documents, and what you need the facility to do.

02

Findings and options

A written summary of what we found, the realistic options, and where money can be saved or risk reduced.

03

Start-to-finish project plan

The stages of the project in order, from design and procurement to installation, testing and handover.

04

Reference costing

An indicative cost reference for planning and budgeting, prepared from the information available at the time.

05

How the work was done

A plain explanation of the method, assumptions and standards behind the advice, so your team or another contractor can follow it.

Fees

How pricing works

Consultancy is a paid service and is not free. The fee depends on your site, your sector, the size of the facility and the stage of the project, so there is no single price list.

Send a request and we will reply with a written quotation in Saudi riyals stating the scope, the deliverables and the fee. Nothing starts until you accept it in writing.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Which airport systems can the consultancy look at?

Power backup, lighting, fire protection and HVAC are the main areas, chosen according to what your facility needs. The scope is agreed in writing.

Is the consultancy free?

No. Consultancy is a paid service. We quote a fee in writing after we receive your request, and work starts only after you accept it.

Is the reference costing a binding quotation?

No. It is an indicative reference for planning and budgeting. A binding price arises only from a signed contract or purchase order.

Do I have to buy equipment or installation from Black Arrow?

No. The consultancy is a separate service. You may use the plan with any contractor.

Send your request and we will reply with the scope and fee.
