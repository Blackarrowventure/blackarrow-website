Diversified Supply

What Our General Trading Division Does

Alongside our core electrical and infrastructure services, we operate as a general trading company sourcing and supplying equipment across several sectors — industrial machinery and consumables, safety equipment, aviation-specific products, oil & gas fittings, electrical components, and IT hardware. This lets clients consolidate multiple procurement needs with one supplier they already trust.

We work with manufacturers and distributors across each sector to source genuine equipment at competitive pricing, backed by the same responsiveness and technical support our infrastructure clients already rely on — in alignment with Saudi Vision 2030's push toward diversified, reliable local supply chains.
