# Google Business Profile — ready-to-paste setup pack

Creating this listing is the single highest-value local-SEO action available to you, and it is free.
For a Dammam contractor, the Google Maps "map pack" is where facility managers and procurement
staff actually click.

**Everything below is written to match the website exactly.** Google cross-checks a Business
Profile against your site, and mismatched name/address/phone (NAP) actively suppresses local
ranking. The site previously said "Al Khobar" in the footer, "Dammam" in the schema markup, and
gave two contradictory sets of opening hours. That has been fixed sitewide — do not reintroduce a
variation here.

Start at **business.google.com** → *Manage now*.

## 6. Photos — this matters more than people expect

Listings with photos get materially more clicks, and Google favours actively-maintained profiles.

**Upload at minimum:**
- Logo (square, 720×720 — use `assets/images/black-arrow-logo.png`)
- Cover photo (landscape, 1024×576)
- 5–10 real project photos: installed panels, EV chargers, UPS rooms, lighting installations
- 2–3 team/vehicle photos

**Do not** upload stock imagery. Google's photo review can flag it, and buyers can tell.
Photos of your actual installed work outperform stock every time. Strip location EXIF if a site
is confidential.

---

## 7. Reviews — the biggest ranking lever after category

You currently have no testimonials anywhere. Reviews fix both that and your map-pack ranking.

1. Get your review link from the profile dashboard (*Ask for reviews*).
2. Send it to 5–10 satisfied clients — hospital facility managers and contractors are ideal.
3. Reply to **every** review, positive or negative. Response rate is itself a signal.
4. Never buy reviews or incentivise them. It is a suspension risk and it is obvious.

A good ask: *"If you were happy with the isolated power panel work, would you mind leaving a short
Google review? It genuinely helps other hospitals find us."*

---

## 8. After verification

- **Posts:** publish a short update every 2–4 weeks — a completed project, a service, a standards
  note. Posts expire after 7 days for events but keep the profile active.
- **Q&A:** seed 3–5 genuine questions and answer them yourself. This is allowed and it pre-empts
  the questions buyers actually have.
- **Messaging:** only enable it if someone will genuinely respond quickly. A slow reply time is
  displayed publicly.

## 10. One caveat worth knowing

You are competing for your own brand name. `blackarrow-ksa.com` — Black Arrow General Contracting
EST., Al Khobar, registered 2010 — is in the same region and the same mechanical/electrical space,
one hyphen away from your domain. There is also a Black Arrow Trading Est. in Al Khobar.

A verified Business Profile is the fastest way to own "Black Arrow" *in Dammam* specifically, even
while the broader brand term stays contested. That is a realistic goal; outranking a 14-year-old
competitor on the bare brand name is not, at least not quickly.
