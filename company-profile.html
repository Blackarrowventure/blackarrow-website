01 — About Us

Who We Are

Black Arrow Venture is a Saudi-based trading and solutions provider delivering comprehensive infrastructure and technology solutions. Built on a commitment to excellence, innovation and reliability, we serve commercial, industrial, hospitality, healthcare and public-infrastructure sectors.

Our team brings deep expertise in infrastructure design, implementation and long-term support. We specialize in turnkey solutions that integrate supply, professional installation, testing, commissioning and maintenance — every project managed end-to-end.

Operating in alignment with Saudi Vision 2030, we support the Kingdom's transformation toward sustainable energy, smart infrastructure and economic growth.
