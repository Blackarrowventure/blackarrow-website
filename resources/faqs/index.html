Isolated Power Panels

What is an isolated power panel (IPP) and why do hospitals need one?

An isolated power panel is a hospital-grade electrical system required in operating rooms and other wet procedure locations under NFPA 99. It electrically isolates the power supply so a single ground fault doesn't interrupt power to life-critical equipment — a line isolation monitor alerts staff to the fault instead of cutting power outright.

Which IPP brands does Black Arrow supply?

We supply Bender isolated power panels (we are not an authorized or exclusive Bender dealer) and PG LifeLink IPP panels. Our designs are also compatible with Schneider Electric-style isolated power system specifications where a project calls for it.

What standards do your isolated power panels comply with?

Our IPP installations are engineered to NFPA 99, NFPA 70, and applicable SASO requirements for installed electrical equipment in Saudi Arabia.

UPS Solutions

What size UPS system do I need?

It depends on your critical load and required runtime. We start with a load assessment to size the correct UPS capacity (we install systems from 1kVA up to 1MVA) and battery bank, rather than recommending a size before understanding your actual load profile.

What's the difference between online, line-interactive, and standby UPS?

Online (double-conversion) UPS continuously conditions power and offers the highest protection, suited to mission-critical loads. Line-interactive UPS regulates voltage and switches to battery on outage, a good middle-ground option. Standby UPS is the simplest and most economical, switching to battery only when mains power fails outright. We recommend the right topology based on your equipment's sensitivity and your budget.
