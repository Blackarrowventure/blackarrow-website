Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2

3,180 SAR

The Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2 is an enclosed CoreXY 3D printer suited to anyone who wants speed, quality, and multi-color printing at a competitive price. An excellent choice for projects, prototypes, custom products, figurines, and functional parts. It comes with the CANVAS system for multi-color printing with up to 4 filaments, a hardened-steel nozzle rated for high temperatures, a practical build volume, and strong speed suited to daily use and light-to-medium production. Why choose it: multi-color printing via the CANVAS system (up to 4 filaments), high nozzle temperature up to 350°C for a wider range of materials, strong speed up to 500 mm/s with acceleration up to 20,000 mm/s², a practical 256 mm cubed build volume suited to most projects, business-friendly for producing models, products, gifts, and custom parts, and an easy experience with a 5-inch touchscreen, Wi-Fi, and automatic bed leveling. A strong option for anyone who wants an enclosed, fast printer that supports color and practical materials, suited to projects and daily production.
