3D Printing Service in Saudi Arabia

Have a design you want printed? Send it to Black Arrow 3D and we will print it for you. We handle custom gifts, prototypes, decor and artwork, personalized products, customized parts and small batches, and we deliver across Saudi Arabia.

Get Your Design Printed

What you can send us

  • Your own design file
  • A MakerWorld link
  • A sketch or an idea, and we will discuss it with you
  • A photo or reference of what you want

What we print

  • Custom gifts and personalized products
  • Prototypes and rapid prototyping
  • Decor and artwork
  • Customized parts
  • Small-batch production

How it works

  1. Send us your design, MakerWorld link or idea on WhatsApp and ask for a quote.
  2. We confirm all the details with you before production begins, including the file, material, colour, size and timing for your order.
  3. We print your order. 3D printed items are typically prepared within 3 to 5 business days, as most are made to order.
  4. We deliver across Saudi Arabia: standard shipping 30 SAR (4-5 business days) or fast shipping 50 SAR (2-3 business days). You receive a tracking number once your order has shipped.

Good to know

  • Cancellation is possible only before production begins.
  • Custom and personalized orders are not returnable for change of mind. Defects, a wrong product or shipping damage are covered by our Return & Exchange Policy.
  • Payment: Cash on Delivery or Bank Transfer.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a price for a custom print?

Send your design, MakerWorld link or idea on WhatsApp and ask for a 3D printing quote. We confirm the details with you before production.

How long does a custom order take?

3D printed items are typically prepared within 3 to 5 business days, then shipped: standard shipping takes 4-5 business days and fast shipping 2-3 business days.

Do you deliver outside Saudi Arabia?

No. We deliver across Saudi Arabia only.

Can I cancel a custom order?

Only before production begins. Once production has started, cancellation is not available.
