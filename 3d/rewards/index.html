How it works

  1. Create or sign in to your Black Arrow 3D account and add your phone number.
  2. Shop normally — every order adds to your reward balance, shown in SAR.
  3. From your next order onward, use your balance toward what you buy.
  4. Reach 5,000 SAR lifetime spend for Gold, 8,000 SAR for Platinum — your status never resets, even after you redeem.
  5. Gold and Platinum members also get free delivery, and Platinum members a surprise 3D-printed gift with select orders.
