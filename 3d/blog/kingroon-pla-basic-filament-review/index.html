KINGROON PLA Basic Filament Review: Settings, Colours and Who It Suits

KINGROON PLA Basic is an everyday 1.75 mm PLA sold in 1 kg spools. It is the filament we recommend most often for first prints, gifts, toys and household parts. Here is how to print it well, which colours to pick and what a print really costs.

Quick answer

KINGROON PLA Basic is a good default filament for most printers sold in Saudi Arabia, including the Bambu Lab A1, A1 mini, A2L and P2S. It prints at 190–210 °C on a 50–70 °C bed, needs no enclosure, has almost no smell and comes in plenty of colours. Choose it for decorative pieces, gifts, prototypes and indoor parts. Choose PETG instead for parts that sit in a hot car, outdoors or near heat.

Settings that work

  • Bambu Lab printers: in Bambu Studio choose a Generic PLA profile. Run the flow calibration once for a new spool if your printer offers it.
  • Temperature: start at 200 °C. Go towards 210 °C if layers look weak or the surface is dull; go towards 195 °C if you see stringing.
  • Bed: 55–60 °C on a textured or smooth PEI plate. Clean the plate with warm water and dish soap when the first layer stops sticking. Our first-layer guide covers the rest.
  • Cooling: part fan at full speed after the first few layers. PLA likes cooling; that is what keeps overhangs and small details sharp.
  • Speed: the default profiles of modern printers are fine. If very thin walls look rough, lower the outer wall speed rather than the whole print.

Saudi heat and humidity

PLA softens at roughly 55–60 °C. That is fine inside a home or office, but a car dashboard in a Saudi summer gets much hotter, so a PLA phone holder left in the car can warp. For anything that lives in a car or outdoors, use PETG.

PLA also absorbs moisture slowly from the air. In humid coastal cities like Jeddah, Dammam and Khobar, keep opened spools in a sealed box or bag with silica gel. Signs of a wet spool are popping or crackling at the nozzle, extra stringing and a rough surface. Read our filament in Saudi heat and humidity guide for drying and storage.

PLA Basic or PLA+?

The store also sells PLA+ filament. PLA+ is a modified, slightly tougher PLA: a little less brittle, a little better on parts that clip or snap together. For decorative prints and gifts, PLA Basic is all you need. Our PLA vs PLA+ guide explains when the upgrade is worth it.

Frequently asked questions

Does KINGROON PLA work with the Bambu Lab A1 mini?

Yes. It is standard 1.75 mm PLA. Use a Generic PLA profile in Bambu Studio and print at around 200 °C.

Can I use KINGROON PLA in the AMS lite?

Yes, standard 1 kg spools of PLA feed through the AMS lite. Keep the spool winding neat and the filament end clipped into the spool edge when you store it, so it does not tangle. See our AMS lite filament guide.

Is PLA safe for food?

Treat any 3D printed part as not food-safe: the layer lines trap bacteria and colourants are not certified. Use printed cups and plates for decoration only.

How long does a spool last?

A 1 kg spool makes around 100 small prints such as keychains and stands, or 4–5 large vases. Most hobby users finish a spool in a few weeks.
