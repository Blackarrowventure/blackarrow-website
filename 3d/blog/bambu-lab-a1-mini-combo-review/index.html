Bambu Lab A1 mini Combo Review: Price & Specs in Saudi Arabia

The A1 mini Combo pairs Bambu Lab's compact, self-calibrating printer with the AMS lite, so it prints in up to 4 colors from day one. Here is what you get in Saudi Arabia, what it does well, where it falls short, and what to compare it with.

Quick answer

The Bambu Lab A1 mini Combo is our top pick for a first 3D printer. At 2,699 SAR you get a printer that calibrates itself, prints PLA, PETG and TPU reliably, and switches between 4 spools for multi-color prints. Its limits are the 180 mm build size and the open frame, which rules out ABS and ASA.

What it does well

  • No manual tuning. It levels the bed, compensates for vibration and calibrates flow on its own, which removes the main cause of failed first prints.
  • Multi-color from day one. The AMS lite switches between 4 spools automatically, so 2- to 4-color keychains, name plates and figurines need no extra work.
  • Easy software. Pick a model on MakerWorld in the Bambu Handy app and print it from your phone, or slice your own in Bambu Studio.
  • Small and light. At 5.5 kg it fits on a desk or a shelf, and it is quiet enough for a home office.
  • Reliability features: filament run-out sensor, tangle detection and power-loss recovery.

Limits to know

  • 180 mm per side. Big parts need splitting. If that is most of your printing, look at the A2L.
  • Open frame, 80 °C bed. Not for ABS or ASA. Choose an enclosed printer for car parts that sit in the sun.
  • Color changes create purge waste. Every switch purges a little filament, so very color-heavy prints use more material and take longer.
  • The AMS lite is open. Spools sit in the room air, so in humid cities such as Jeddah, Dammam and Khobar store spools sealed when you are not printing.

Verdict

If you want your first prints to work, and to print in color without learning to tune a printer, the A1 mini Combo is the safest buy in Saudi Arabia today. Get the A2L if you know you will print large, and an enclosed printer if you need ABS or ASA. Once it arrives, follow our 7-step setup guide.

FAQ

How much is the Bambu Lab A1 mini Combo in Saudi Arabia?

At Black Arrow 3D it is 2,699 SAR including VAT, with free delivery.

What does the AMS lite do?

It holds 4 spools and feeds them into the printer automatically, so one print can use up to 4 colors or materials.

Is the A1 mini good for beginners?

Yes. It calibrates itself and prints from the phone app, which makes it one of the easiest printers to start with.

Can the A1 mini print PETG and TPU?

Yes. It prints PLA, PETG, TPU and PVA. ABS and ASA are not recommended because it is an open-frame printer.
