Bambu Lab A1 vs A1 mini vs A2L: Which One Should You Buy?

All three are open-frame Bambu Lab printers with the same easy app, automatic calibration and AMS lite for 4-color printing. The real decision is how big your prints are, how much desk space you have, and your budget.

Quick answer

  • A1 mini if most of what you print fits in an 18 cm cube, or desk space is tight. The A1 mini Combo is 2,699 SAR.
  • A2L if you print helmets, cosplay parts, storage boxes or several items per plate. The A2L is 3,350 SAR, or 4,300 SAR as a Combo.
  • A1 sits in between at 256 mm per side. Check its stock line below: when it is out of stock, the A2L is the next size up.

What all three have in common

  • Fully automatic calibration: bed leveling, vibration compensation and flow calibration, so there is nothing to tune by hand.
  • An all-metal hot end up to 300 °C, with quick-swap nozzles from 0.2 to 0.8 mm.
  • The same software: Bambu Studio on a computer and the Bambu Handy app, with free models on MakerWorld.
  • An open frame. They are made for PLA, PETG and TPU. For ABS or ASA choose an enclosed printer such as the P1S or the Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2.

Bambu Lab A1 mini: smallest footprint, lowest price

The A1 mini fits on a normal desk and covers most first projects: keychains, phone stands, figurines, organisers and gifts. Its 180 mm limit is the trade-off: helmets, large vases and long parts need splitting into pieces. Read our A1 mini Combo review.

Bambu Lab A1: the middle size

The A1 gives you a 256 mm cube, the same build size as the enclosed P1S and P2S, with a 100 °C bed. It is the balanced choice for household parts and medium cosplay props when it is in stock.

Bambu Lab A2L: for genuinely large prints

The A2L prints up to 330 x 320 x 325 mm, about twice the volume of the A1, with a built-in filament cutter. It suits full helmets, large storage boxes and batch production. It needs a sturdy table about 55 cm square. See our A2L Combo guide.

Printer only or Combo?

The Combo adds the AMS lite, which holds 4 spools and switches between them during a print. Choose it if you want multi-color gifts, signs or figurines. If you only ever print one color at a time, the printer alone is enough, and you can still change filament by hand. Learn more in multi-color 3D printing explained.

FAQ

What is the difference between the A1 and A1 mini?

Size. The A1 prints up to 256 mm per side and the A1 mini up to 180 mm. The A1 also has a hotter bed (100 °C vs 80 °C). Speed, software and the AMS lite are the same.

Is the A2L bigger than the A1?

Yes. The A2L prints up to 330 x 320 x 325 mm, against 256 x 256 x 256 mm on the A1.

Which Bambu Lab printer is best for beginners?

The A1 mini Combo, for most people. See the best 3D printer for beginners.

Can these printers print ABS?

They are open-frame printers, so ABS and ASA are not recommended. Use an enclosed printer for those materials.
