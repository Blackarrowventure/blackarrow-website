Bambu Lab P1S vs P2S: Which Enclosed Printer to Buy in Saudi Arabia

The P2S replaces the P1S as Bambu Lab's enclosed P-series printer, but the P1S is still on sale for less. Both have the same 256 mm build volume. Here is what the extra money buys you and which one makes sense in Saudi Arabia.

Quick answer

Buy the P1S (3,499 SAR) if you want a proven enclosed printer for ABS, ASA, PLA and PETG at the lower price. Buy the P2S if you want the newer hardened-steel toolhead, a touchscreen and the AMS 2 Pro: the Combo is 6,299 SAR. Print size is the same on both, and reviewers like Hackster saw no major difference in print quality.

What the P2S changes

  • Hardened steel from day one. The P2S ships with a hardened-steel nozzle and extruder gears, so carbon- and glass-fiber filaments work without upgrades. Bambu Lab does not recommend fiber filaments on the P1S until the extruder and hot end are upgraded.
  • A new toolhead and touchscreen. Reviewers highlight the new servo-driven extruder and the touchscreen interface that replaces the P1S buttons.
  • Better cooling and a hotter bed. Reviewers report improved airflow for materials that needed the door open on the P1S, and the bed now reaches 110 °C.
  • Bigger multi-color setups. The AMS 2 Pro can dry filament, and the P2S connects up to 8 AMS units for up to 20 colors.

What stays the same

  • The 256 x 256 x 256 mm build volume, so the same models fit.
  • Top speed and acceleration.
  • An enclosed chamber suited to ABS and ASA.
  • Bambu Studio and the Bambu Handy app.

Which one should you buy?

  • Choose the P1S for the lowest price on an enclosed Bambu Lab printer, especially if you print mostly PLA, PETG, ABS and ASA. Add an AMS later for colors.
  • Choose the P2S Combo if you will print fiber-filled filaments, want multi-color with filament drying from day one, or run a small print business where the newer toolhead and interface save time.
  • Already own a P1S that works well? Many owners keep it. The upgrade is about convenience and materials, not print size or quality.

On a tighter budget, the Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2 is also enclosed and includes 4-color printing. More detail is in our P2S Combo review.

FAQ

Is the P2S better than the P1S?

It is newer, with a hardened-steel toolhead, touchscreen, better cooling and a 110 °C bed. Build size, speed and print quality are close, so the P1S is still a good buy for less money.

Do the P1S and P2S have the same build volume?

Yes, both print up to 256 x 256 x 256 mm.

Can the P1S print carbon-fiber filament?

Only after upgrading to a hardened-steel extruder and hot end, according to Bambu Lab. The P2S can print it out of the box.

How much are the P1S and P2S in Saudi Arabia?

At Black Arrow 3D the P1S is 3,499 SAR and the P2S Combo is 6,299 SAR, VAT included.
