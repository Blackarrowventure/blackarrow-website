Best Filament for the Bambu Lab A1, A1 mini and AMS lite

The Bambu Lab A1 series is the most popular printer family in Saudi Arabia, and most owners use the AMS lite for multi-colour prints. This guide covers which filaments work best, which ones to keep out of the AMS lite, and how to avoid feeding problems.

Quick answer

For the A1 mini, A1 and A2L, PLA is the best everyday filament and the easiest in the AMS lite. Add PETG for heat- and water-resistant parts and PLA+ for tougher functional prints. Keep flexible TPU out of the AMS lite: feed it directly from the external spool holder. The A1 series is an open printer, so ABS and ASA are not a good match.

Feeding tips for the AMS lite

  • Neat winding: tangles cause most AMS lite jams. When you remove a spool, always clip the end into the spool edge.
  • Spool size: standard 1 kg spools fit. Very wide or very small spools may not turn freely; check before loading.
  • Cardboard spools: they can shed dust and slip on the rollers. If you use them often, a plastic spool adapter ring helps.
  • Cut the end straight with a sharp angle before loading so it does not catch inside the feeder.
  • Tell the printer what is loaded: set the material and colour for each slot in Bambu Studio or Bambu Handy so the right temperature is used.

Multi-colour without wasting a spool

Every colour change purges some filament. To keep waste down:

  1. Put colours that change most often on the same layers, and use fewer colour changes per layer.
  2. Turn on flush into infill or flush into object where the inside of the print is hidden.
  3. Use darker colours after lighter ones where you can: going from white to black purges less than black to white.

A starter set we recommend

For a new A1 or A1 mini owner: black and white KINGROON PLA Basic for most prints, two bright colours for multi-colour gifts, and one spool of KINGROON PETG Basic for parts that go in the car. Keep a spare A1 hot end on hand; it is the part that wears with use.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to use Bambu Lab filament on an A1?

No. Any good 1.75 mm filament works. Bambu filament has RFID tags that load settings automatically; with other brands you choose the profile yourself, which takes a few seconds.

Can the AMS lite mix PLA and PETG in one print?

It can load both, but they do not bond well to each other, so use one material per part. PLA works well as an easy-release support under PETG.

Why does my AMS lite fail to load filament?

Usually a tangle on the spool, a bent filament end or a spool that does not turn freely. Re-cut the end, rewind loose loops and make sure the spool spins easily.
