3D Printed Gifts for Saudi Occasions: A Calendar Guide

Every occasion in the Saudi calendar has a gift that fits it, from a 10 SAR keychain to a custom logo piece for a whole company. Here is what to give for each one, and how early to order.

Quick answer: Order 3D printed gifts at least 10 business days before the occasion. Our printed items are made to order in 3–5 business days, then delivered across Saudi Arabia in 4–5 business days with Standard delivery (30 SAR, free on 100 SAR or more of 3D printed items) or 2–3 business days with Fast delivery (50 SAR). For Saudi occasions, the best-value picks are currently the Saudi Pride Gift Set (35 SAR), the Saudi Map Keychain (10 SAR on offer) and the Saudi Map Hanging Decor (25 SAR). For a name or logo, Custom 3D Artwork starts at 25 SAR.

This guide is organized by occasion. If you would rather browse by product type, see 3D printed gifts and home decor ideas.

Ramadan and both Eids follow the Hijri calendar, so they move about 11 days earlier each year in the Gregorian calendar. Check the expected dates and count back from them.

Saudi National Day (23 September)

National Day is the biggest gifting moment for local-themed pieces. The Saudi National Day Key Holder (25 SAR, keychain included) was designed for National Day 96 in 2026. Pair it with the Saudi Map Keychain, Palm & Swords Keychain or Saudi Riyal Keychain, each currently 10 SAR on offer (regular price 20 SAR) in green and other colors. For schools, offices and events, National Day giveaways are one of the most common orders on our business and bulk orders page, so order in August or early September.

Founding Day (22 February)

Founding Day celebrates the roots of the Saudi state, and the palm and crossed swords design fits it perfectly. The Saudi Pride Gift Set combines the Saudi Map Keychain, the Palm & Swords Keychain and the National Day Key Holder for 35 SAR. The Saudi Map Hanging Decor (25 SAR), a small map with articulated legs that dangle over the edge of a shelf or monitor, makes a fun desk gift for colleagues. See the full National Day and Founding Day gift collection.

Ramadan

Ramadan gifts are often for the home and for the evenings. The Synapse USB Lamp (60 SAR) has a circuit-style shade that glows in warm tones and runs from any USB port, and the Air Filter Style LED Lamp (65 SAR without light, 85 SAR with light) suits a car enthusiast. For a personal touch, a crescent, lantern or family name can be made as a Custom 3D Artwork piece.

Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha

Eid usually means many small gifts at once: for children, cousins and guests. Keychains at 10 SAR each are ideal for this, and mixing a few Saudi-themed keychains with small fidget pieces, such as the Gear Fidget Spinner (15 SAR) or the Infinity Cube Fidget Toy (30 SAR), gives children something to play with. For close family, a display piece such as the F1 Race Car Display Model (50 SAR) or the Mustang GT model (30 SAR) feels more special. Browse gifts under 50 SAR for more ideas.

Graduation

Graduation gifts should last beyond the ceremony. Overthing (100 SAR), a sculpture of a large hand holding a figure on fine strings, is a thoughtful conversation piece for a graduate’s desk. The Synapse USB Lamp suits engineering and computer science graduates. A name, degree or graduation year plaque can be ordered as Custom 3D Artwork: Medium (65 SAR) covers a desk piece. For students who are still working on their own projects, see 3D printing for students and engineers.

Weddings and birthdays

For weddings, custom pieces work best: the couple’s initials, a date plaque, or table name stands for the event. Initials and plaques can be ordered as Custom 3D Artwork, and event items such as name stands and table signs in quantity go through the business page. For birthdays, match the gift to the person: lamps and sculptures for adults, and for gamers there is a whole gamers gift collection with controller stands such as the Pillow Controller Stand (50 SAR) and the football PS5 Dual Controller Stand (80 SAR).

Corporate gifts

Companies, government entities and event organizers order logo keychains, plaques, awards, desk pieces and National Day or Founding Day giveaways. Send the item, quantity, logo and the date you need it by, and we reply with a quote and a digital mock-up. There is no fixed minimum, the price per piece drops as quantity goes up, larger runs start with a sample for approval, and we provide a VAT invoice in your company name. Start on the corporate gifts and bulk orders page.

Custom names and logos

Custom 3D Artwork is priced by size: Small (keychain or badge) 25 SAR, Medium (desk piece) 65 SAR and Large (display sculpture) 150 SAR. Pieces come in a raw, unpainted PLA finish. Choose a size, send your reference image on WhatsApp (+966 56 022 4715), and we confirm the details before printing. If you already have a 3D file, the 3D printing service and instant STL quote are the faster route. Note that custom orders can be cancelled only before production begins and are not returnable for change of mind.

Ordering, delivery and payment

  • Made to order: printed items take 3–5 business days, then ship from Dammam.
  • Delivery: Standard 4–5 business days (30 SAR) or Fast 2–3 business days (50 SAR), anywhere in Saudi Arabia.
  • Free regular delivery on 100 SAR or more of 3D printed items. Example: a Synapse USB Lamp (60 SAR), a Saudi Pride Gift Set (35 SAR) and a Saudi Map Keychain (10 SAR) come to 105 SAR.
  • Payment: card (Visa, Mastercard, mada), Apple Pay, bank transfer, Tabby or Tamara. There is no pay-later service fee on 3D printed items.

Frequently asked questions

How early should I order a 3D printed gift?

At least 10 business days before the occasion with Standard delivery, or about 8 business days with Fast delivery. Allow more time for custom designs and bulk orders.

Can you add a name or logo to a gift?

Yes. Single custom pieces are ordered as Custom 3D Artwork, priced by size from 25 SAR. For logos on many pieces, use the business page and we will send a mock-up first.

What is a good Saudi National Day gift under 50 SAR?

The Saudi Pride Gift Set (35 SAR) with three pieces, the National Day Key Holder (25 SAR) or a Saudi Map Keychain (currently 10 SAR on offer).

Is delivery free on gift orders?

Regular delivery is free when your order includes 100 SAR or more of 3D printed items. Otherwise Standard delivery is 30 SAR and Fast delivery 50 SAR.
