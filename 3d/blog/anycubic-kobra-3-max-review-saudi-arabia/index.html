Anycubic Kobra 3 Max Review: The Big-Format 3D Printer in Saudi Arabia

The Kobra 3 Max is the largest printer we sell: 420 x 420 x 500 mm, enough for full-size helmets, signage and furniture parts in one piece. Here is the price in Saudi Arabia, what reviewers found, and whether you really need that much space.

Quick answer

The Anycubic Kobra 3 Max is for people who print big parts in one piece. Its 420 x 420 x 500 mm build volume is more than twice the plate area of most 256 mm printers, and it costs 2,999 SAR, or 3,920 SAR as a Combo for multi-color printing. If your parts fit in about 330 mm, the Bambu Lab A2L is smaller, lighter and easier to place.

What reviewers liked

  • A big step up from the Kobra 2 Max. Tom's Hardware called it a massive improvement, with reliable automatic leveling, input shaping and decent speed.
  • Leveling that just works. 3DTested said bed leveling is fully automatic and works extremely well.
  • Size and color together. 3DTested described it as a super-sized printer with a four-color spool system that delivers on both quality and speed.

What to know before you buy

  • Measure your space. It needs about 706 x 640 x 753 mm and weighs 19 kg. Put it on a solid table, not a light desk.
  • Bed adhesion on big prints. 3DTested found adhesion a little dicey, easily fixed with glue stick or a different plate. Large flat parts are also more prone to warping.
  • Open frame. It is made for PLA, PETG and TPU. For ABS and ASA choose an enclosed printer such as the Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2.
  • Closed firmware. Tom's Hardware noted there is no access to config files or SSH, though it works fine in practice. This matters only to tinkerers.
  • Big prints take time and filament. A full-height print can run for days; budget several 1 kg spools of filament.

FAQ

How much is the Anycubic Kobra 3 Max in Saudi Arabia?

At Black Arrow 3D it is 2,999 SAR, or 3,920 SAR for the Combo, VAT included.

What is the build volume of the Kobra 3 Max?

420 x 420 x 500 mm, the largest of the printers we sell.

Can it print a full-size helmet in one piece?

Most adult helmets fit inside 420 x 420 x 500 mm, so yes, without splitting the model. Check your model's dimensions in the slicer first.

Can the Kobra 3 Max print ABS?

It is an open-frame printer with a 90 °C bed, so it is not a good fit for ABS or ASA. Use an enclosed printer for those.
