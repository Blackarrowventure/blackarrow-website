Bambu Lab vs Creality vs Snapmaker: Which 3D Printer Should You Buy?

We carry printers from all three brands. Here's one representative model from each, compared on what actually matters: build volume, multicolor printing, speed and price.

Bambu Lab A1 — the established all-rounder

The Bambu Lab A1 has a 256 x 256 x 256 mm build volume, a 300°C hot end and 100°C bed, with an AMS Lite combo option for up to 4-color printing. It's rated for 500 mm/s max speed and 10,000 mm/s² acceleration. It starts at 2,700 SAR standalone, or 3,400 SAR as a combo, and is in stock and ships now.

Creality SparkX i7 — the budget-friendly newcomer

The Creality SparkX i7 is a close match on paper: 260 x 260 x 255 mm build volume, the same 300°C hot end and 100°C bed ratings, and CFS Lite support for up to 4 filaments. It's priced at 2,090 SAR and is currently a pre-order item.

Snapmaker U1 — four independent toolheads

The Snapmaker U1 takes a different approach to multicolor: instead of an AMS-style filament swap, it uses 4 independent toolheads via its SnapSwap system, which generally means faster color changes with less waste. It has the largest build volume of the three (270 x 270 x 270 mm) and the highest acceleration rating (20,000 mm/s²). At 5,550 SAR it's priced well above the other two, and is also currently a pre-order item.

Which one should you buy?

If budget comes first, the SparkX i7 is the lowest price of the three at 2,090 SAR, though it's currently a pre-order. If you want the most established ecosystem and don't mind paying more for a printer that's in stock today, the Bambu Lab A1 is the straightforward choice at 2,700 SAR standalone (or 3,400 SAR as a combo) — see our guide on how AMS, CANVAS and SnapSwap compare for more on multicolor systems specifically. The Snapmaker U1 is the one to pick if 4-toolhead speed matters more to you than price, again as a pre-order.

A note on pre-orders: both the SparkX i7 and the U1 are pre-order items right now. If you order one, we'll confirm your order with you about a week before delivery, same as any other pre-order on the site.
