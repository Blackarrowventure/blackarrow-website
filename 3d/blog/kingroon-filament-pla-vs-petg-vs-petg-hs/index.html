KINGROON PLA vs PETG vs PETG HS: Which KINGROON Filament Should You Buy?

KINGROON makes three everyday filaments we stock: PLA Basic, PETG Basic and the new PETG HS high-speed. All three are 1.75 mm, 1 kg and 65 SAR. Here is what separates them, which one suits which print, and the colours that just arrived.

Quick answer

  • PLA Basic for most prints: gifts, toys, figures, decor and prototypes. Easiest to print, stiffest, most colours.
  • PETG Basic for parts that get warm, wet or knocked: clips, hooks, plant pots, covers. Glossy finish, lots of colours including transparent.
  • PETG HS for the same jobs as PETG, printed fast and with a matte finish. Best on high-speed printers.

All three are 65 SAR per 1 kg spool at Black Arrow 3D, VAT included.

FAQ

What is the difference between KINGROON PETG Basic and PETG HS?

PETG HS is KINGROON's high-speed PETG: KINGROON says it prints up to twice as fast as standard PETG by default, with less stringing, and it has a matte finish instead of PETG Basic's gloss.

Which KINGROON filament is best for beginners?

PLA Basic. It prints at lower temperatures, sticks easily on a 35–45 °C bed and forgives most mistakes.

Which KINGROON filament handles Saudi heat best?

Either PETG. In KINGROON's data PETG HS deflects at 62 °C against 54 °C for PLA Basic. No common filament is safe on a dashboard in direct summer sun, though.
