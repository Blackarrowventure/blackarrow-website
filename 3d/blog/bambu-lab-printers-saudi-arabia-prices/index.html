Bambu Lab Printers in Saudi Arabia: Prices and Lineup (2026)

Black Arrow 3D sells ten Bambu Lab printers in Saudi Arabia, from the compact A1 mini to the professional H2C. Here are the current SAR prices, what is in stock, and which one fits which kind of maker.

Quick answer

As of October 2026, Black Arrow 3D lists ten Bambu Lab printers. In stock now: the A1 mini Combo at 2,699 SAR, the A2L at 3,250 SAR (4,200 SAR as a Combo), the P1S at 3,499 SAR, the P2S Combo at 6,299 SAR, the X2D AMS Combo at 7,899 SAR, the X1E Combo at 11,999 SAR and the H2C at 15,990 SAR. The H2S AMS Combo (9,999 SAR) and the H2D AMS Combo (13,999 SAR) are available to pre-order. The A1 (2,700 SAR, or 3,449 SAR as a Combo) and the standalone A1 mini (1,699 SAR) and P2S (4,399 SAR) are currently out of stock. All prices include VAT. For most beginners the A1 mini Combo is the easiest start; the A2L suits anyone who needs bigger prints; the P1S and P2S are the enclosed choices for daily production; the X2D and H2D print two materials in one job; and the H2S, X1E and H2C are for large or engineering-grade work.

Bambu Lab A1 mini: the compact starter

The A1 mini is a compact FDM printer with a 180 x 180 x 180 mm build volume, an all-metal hot end rated to 300 °C, an 80 °C bed and speeds up to 500 mm/s. It has automatic bed leveling, a filament run-out sensor, power-loss recovery and filament tangle detection, and connects over Wi-Fi. It prints PLA, PETG, TPU and PVA, weighs 5.5 kg and draws up to 150 W on 100–240 VAC input.

Who it is for: first-time owners, students and anyone printing keychains, small figures and desk items. The Combo adds the AMS lite for multi-color prints. Read our A1 mini Combo review.

Bambu Lab A1: more room for the home

The A1 offers a 256 x 256 x 256 mm build volume, a 300 °C hot end, a 100 °C bed, 500 mm/s top speed and AMS Lite support for up to 4 colors. It is currently out of stock in both versions (2,700 SAR standalone, 3,449 SAR Combo); the product page shows when it is back. Read our A1 Combo review.

Who it is for: beginners who want a larger bed than the A1 mini for helmets, organisers and bigger models.

Bambu Lab A2L Combo: large-format, still easy

The A2L is the large-format member of the A-series. Its 330 x 320 x 325 mm build volume (about 34.3 liters) is more than double the A1’s. It keeps fully automatic calibration, including flow-dynamics calibration before each print, a quick-swap 0.4 mm nozzle (0.2 to 0.8 mm available), a built-in filament cutter and a touchscreen. The Combo includes the AMS lite for up to four colors or materials. Top speed is 500 mm/s with 10,000 mm/s² acceleration, a 300 °C hot end and an 80 °C bed. It is best suited to PLA, PETG and TPU; ABS and ASA are not recommended.

The A2L costs 3,250 SAR as a printer only, or 4,200 SAR as a Combo with the AMS lite.

Who it is for: cosplay makers, home decor, bigger models and small businesses that print large items. Compare it in A1 vs A1 mini vs A2L.

Bambu Lab P1S: the proven enclosed printer

The P1S is the enclosed P-series printer with a 256 x 256 x 256 mm build volume, a 300 °C hot end, a 100 °C bed, 500 mm/s top speed and 20,000 mm/s² acceleration, with an activated-carbon filter. We sell it as a printer only at 3,499 SAR; add an AMS any time for multi-color printing.

Who it is for: makers who want an enclosed printer for ABS and ASA at a lower price than the P2S.

Bambu Lab P2S Combo: enclosed daily production

The P2S is an enclosed, fast printer built for everyday production. It has a 256 x 256 x 256 mm build volume, a 300 °C hot end, a 110 °C bed, 500 mm/s top speed and 20,000 mm/s² acceleration, and connects via Wi-Fi with Bambu Handy and Bambu Studio. It weighs 14.7 kg.

Who it is for: workshops, sellers and anyone running repeat orders who wants an enclosed machine. The Combo adds the AMS 2 Pro for multi-color printing.

Bambu Lab X2D AMS Combo: two nozzles on a desk

The X2D is a compact, enclosed dual-nozzle printer with a 256 x 256 x 260 mm build volume, a 120 °C bed, an actively heated chamber up to 65 °C and 1,000 mm/s top speed. Two nozzles print two materials or colors in one job with much less waste. The AMS Combo (7,899 SAR) includes the AMS 2 Pro.

Who it is for: designers and small businesses who want dual-material prints, such as dissolvable supports, without a large machine.

Bambu Lab H2S AMS Combo: big single-nozzle prints

The H2S has a 340 x 320 x 340 mm build volume, a 350 °C hot end, a 120 °C bed, an actively heated chamber up to 65 °C and 1,000 mm/s top speed. The AMS Combo (9,999 SAR) includes the AMS 2 Pro and is available to pre-order.

Who it is for: anyone printing large cosplay, props and prototypes in one piece, including engineering materials.

Bambu Lab X1E Combo: built for companies

The X1E is the enterprise edition of the X1 series: a 256 x 256 x 256 mm build volume, a 320 °C hardened-steel hot end, a 120 °C bed, an actively heated chamber up to 60 °C, and Ethernet with WPA2-Enterprise and LAN-only mode for business networks. The Combo (11,999 SAR) includes the AMS.

Who it is for: companies, schools and labs that print engineering materials on a secured network.

Bambu Lab H2D AMS Combo: the dual-nozzle flagship

The H2D prints with two nozzles across a 350 x 320 x 325 mm build volume, with a 350 °C hot end, a 120 °C bed, an actively heated chamber up to 65 °C and 1,000 mm/s top speed. The AMS Combo (13,999 SAR) includes the AMS 2 Pro and is available to pre-order.

Who it is for: studios and workshops that print large multi-material parts and engineering materials.

Bambu Lab H2C: professional tier

The H2C is a professional-tier, enclosed printer with a 330 x 320 x 325 mm build volume, a 350 °C hot end, chamber temperatures up to 65 °C and a top speed of 1,000 mm/s. It handles PLA, PETG, TPU, ABS, ASA, PA and similar materials depending on settings, and weighs 32.5 kg. Multi-color and multi-material capability depends on configuration.

Who it is for: workshops, retailers and engineering users who need higher output and advanced materials. It is listed at 15,990 SAR; check the product page for availability.

Combo vs standalone

A standalone printer prints one filament at a time. A Combo bundles the printer with an AMS unit that holds several spools and switches between them automatically, so you can print in multiple colors or materials and swap spools without unloading by hand. On current prices the AMS lite adds 1,000 SAR to the A1 mini (1,699 vs 2,699) and the AMS 2 Pro adds 1,900 SAR to the P2S (4,399 vs 6,299). Right now only the Combo versions of the A1 mini, A1 and P2S are in stock, and the A2L is sold as a Combo.

AMS lite vs AMS 2 Pro

  • AMS lite pairs with the A-series (A1 mini, A1, A2L). It supports up to 4 colors or materials with automatic spool switching.
  • AMS 2 Pro is the multi-color unit in the P2S, X2D, H2S and H2D Combos, and it also dries filament at up to 65 °C. It is sold on its own too (2,299 SAR), as is the single-spool AMS HT (999 SAR), which dries at up to 85 °C.

The simple rule: the AMS unit follows the printer you choose. If you are unsure, read multi-color 3D printing explained or ask us on WhatsApp.
