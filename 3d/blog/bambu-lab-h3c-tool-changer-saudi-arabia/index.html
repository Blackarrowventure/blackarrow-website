Bambu Lab H3C: The 9-Tool Tool Changer, Specs and Price in Saudi Arabia

On 3 November 2026 Bambu Lab revealed the H3C, its first third-generation printer and its first true tool changer. Nine independent PrintTools switch materials in about six seconds, and a new AMS 3H stores and dries filament inside the machine. Here is what it does, the official specs, how it compares with the printers you can buy today, and when it reaches Saudi Arabia.

Quick answer

The Bambu Lab H3C is a multi-material printer built around a tool changer. Instead of one nozzle that purges every time the colour changes, it parks nine complete PrintTools on a rack and picks up the one it needs. Bambu Lab says a material switch takes about 6 seconds with very little purge. It comes with the new AMS 3H, which holds nine spools in a sealed chamber and dries two at up to 85 °C. It is coming soon to Black Arrow 3D at 17,899 SAR (H3C with AMS 3H, VAT included), with delivery across Saudi Arabia.

What is new: a real tool changer

Bambu Lab calls the system Swaptek. Each of the nine PrintTools has its own filament path and its own heating, so a colour change is a tool swap, not a long purge through one hot end. Bambu Lab's Active Pre-Heat warms the next tool before it is needed, which is how it reaches the 6-second switch with low priming. Because each tool is independent, the H3C can also:

  • mix different nozzle sizes in one print (fine detail and fast infill in the same job);
  • use crafting tools Bambu Lab calls CutTool, DrawTool and DebossTool;
  • reach up to 24 materials in one print by connecting an existing Bambu Lab AMS through PrintTool 1.

That is different from the Bambu Lab H2C, which swaps nozzles on its hot end. Reviewers testing the H3C make the same distinction: it is a tool changer, not a nozzle swapper.

AMS 3H: storage, drying and feeding built in

The AMS 3H sits on top of the printer as part of the design. It is a fully enclosed, airtight spool chamber with nine slots, reads each spool by RFID, keeps an inventory of type, colour and remaining weight, and has dedicated inlets for TPU. Two drying slots reach 85 °C, so the next spools can dry while you print. For Saudi buyers in humid cities like Jeddah, Dammam and Khobar, that matters: nylon, TPU and PETG all print better dry. Read our filament in Saudi heat and humidity guide.

FlexFeed: soft TPU without the fight

The FlexFeed extruder takes both 1.75 mm and 2.85 mm filament and switches between them. Bambu Lab says it prints TPU as soft as 75A at up to three times the flow of a classic printer, which opens up shoe soles, grips, seals and gaskets that most multi-colour printers struggle with.

If you need a multi-colour printer now, the Snapmaker U1 is a tool changer for a third of the price, and the H2D and H2C cover engineering materials today. The H3C is for workshops and makers who want the most colours and materials in one print with the least waste.

Who the H3C suits

  • Print shops and product makers printing multi-colour parts every day, where purge waste and switch time add up.
  • Engineers and designers combining rigid and flexible materials, or PLA with soluble supports, in one part.
  • Schools and labs that want one enclosed, filtered machine for many materials.

FAQ

What is the Bambu Lab H3C?

Bambu Lab's first third-generation 3D printer and its first tool changer, with nine independent PrintTools (Swaptek), a material switch of about 6 seconds and the integrated AMS 3H filament system.

How many colours can the H3C print?

Nine from the AMS 3H, one per PrintTool, and up to 24 materials in one print by connecting an existing Bambu Lab AMS through PrintTool 1.

Can the H3C dry filament?

Yes. The AMS 3H has two drying slots that reach 85 °C, so filament can dry while you print.

Does the H3C print TPU?

Yes. Its FlexFeed extruder takes 1.75 mm and 2.85 mm filament, and Bambu Lab says it prints TPU as soft as 75A.

How much is the Bambu Lab H3C in Saudi Arabia?

17,899 SAR including VAT for the H3C with AMS 3H at Black Arrow 3D, coming soon, with delivery across Saudi Arabia.

Is the H3C better than the H2C?

It is faster (1,400 vs 1,000 mm/s) and changes whole tools rather than nozzles, with nine tools and built-in drying. Both share the same 330 × 320 × 325 mm build volume, 350 °C nozzles and 65 °C heated chamber. The H2C is available now and costs less.
